This app an intelligent AI co-pilot for podcast hosts and MCs. It helps you schedule episodes, deeply understand your guests, prepare smart talking points, and — most importantly — gives you real-time suggestions while you’re recording.

1. User Onboarding & Basics

- Simple registration/login (email + Google)
- Create your Host Profile (name, podcast name, hosting style)
- Dashboard: Upcoming episodes + quick actions

2. Schedule & Episode Management (Simple)

- Calendar integration (Google Calendar is enough)
- Create new episode → Set date/time + title + short description
- Basic episode list with status (Upcoming / Recorded / Published)
- Can add like series tagging to the episode

3. Guest Management (Light CRM)

- Add guest (manual or simple import via URL/LinkedIn)
- AI Guest Profile: Upload bio/link → AI auto-generates summary, key topics, suggested angles
- Guest list with search and basic notes

4. Episode Preparation (The Pre-Show Intelligence)

- Upload materials (PDFs, articles, links, notes)
- AI Analysis: Summarizes materials + guest profile → Generates:Episode outline
- 8–15 tailored questions
- Key talking points & potential follow-ups

5. Recording + Realtime AI Co-Pilot (My Killer Feature)

- Live transcription (speaker detection)
- Realtime Sidebar Suggestions (this is what makes it special):Suggested next questions
- Follow-up ideas based on what was just said
- Topic transition prompts
- Fact-check alerts (light)

6. Basic Post-Recording

- View transcript + recording
- Upload Video
- Simple AI-generated show notes & chapter timestamps
- Export clipped video + subtitl
